# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want a quick, playful celebrity lookalike result from a photo, plus visitors who need to contact the small team about bugs, privacy, photo removal, or legal questions.

## Product Purpose

Ollie lets someone upload a face photo, compare it with a large celebrity image index, and explore the closest matches. The primary workflow should feel immediate, curious, and trustworthy, with clear privacy expectations and a simple path between Home, Match, and Contact.

## Brand Personality

Playful, electric, curious. Match and Contact should feel like natural extensions of the current Home experience, with smooth restrained motion and no changes to Home itself.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, dense utility forms, overly decorative glassmorphism, loud page-load choreography, and navigation that hides the three core destinations.

## Design Principles

- Keep the upload-to-result workflow prominent and easy to resume.
- Use one shared visual language across Home, Match, and Contact.
- Make motion clarify state and scrolling rhythm, never delay the task.
- Keep privacy, credits, and support information easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve keyboard-visible focus, maintain readable contrast, and keep forms and navigation usable on small screens.
